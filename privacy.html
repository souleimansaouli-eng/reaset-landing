Privacy Policy

How Reaset handles and protects your information.

HomeTermsSupport

Effective date: August 2, 2026.

Reaset helps readers track reading, manage books, discover bookstores, join reading circles, and receive reading reminders.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address, name, handle, avatar, profile preferences, and the sign-in provider you choose. If you continue with Google, Google supplies the basic identity details you authorize, such as your name, email address, and profile image.
  • Reading data: books, shelves, reading sessions, goals, favourites, lists, reviews, and questionnaire results.
  • Circle data: circles joined or created, membership, and posts.
  • Notification data: reminder preferences and in-app notification state.
  • Bookstore discovery data: a city or area you search, or your foreground location when you explicitly tap “Near me,” is sent to our bookstore search service to return nearby bookstores. Reaset does not persist these searches or your current-location coordinates. Bookstores you save are stored with your account.
  • Diagnostic data: app version, build number, device model, operating-system version, an anonymous installation identifier, and crash or error details needed for reliability.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide the app, sync data across sessions, restore your library after login, show recommendations, find bookstores in an area you choose, support reading circles, schedule reminders, investigate content reports, and improve reliability.

Storage And Sync

Reaset stores core reading data locally on your device for offline use. When signed in, data syncs with Supabase so it can be restored and kept consistent across sessions. Saved bookstores sync with your account. Bookstore search text and current-location coordinates are not saved to your Reaset account.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Circle posts and public profile information may be visible to other authenticated users according to the circle and profile features you use.

Third-Party Services

Reaset uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage, realtime sync, account deletion processing, and secure service gateways. If you choose Google sign-in, Google authenticates you and provides the basic identity details you authorize; Reaset does not receive your Google password. Reaset uses ISBNdb for book-search results and metadata. Reaset uses Geoapify and OpenStreetMap data to find bookstores in the area you choose. Geoapify processes the search request under its own privacy terms. Reaset uses Sentry for crash and error diagnostics. Sentry is configured without behavioral analytics, session replay, screenshots, view hierarchy, performance tracing, or default personally identifiable information.

Diagnostic reports are filtered to avoid sending account credentials, email addresses, profile names, book notes, reviews, quotes, chat text, search text, and imported file contents.

Your Choices

You can use city or area search without granting location access, deny or revoke location permission in iOS Settings, remove saved bookstores, edit profile information, sign out, export data, and permanently delete your account from the app. Account deletion removes the Supabase Auth account, account-backed database rows, and uploaded avatars or Circle banners. Reaset also cancels the daily reminder and clears account-scoped data, reading positions, and active timers stored by the app on that device. Deletion cannot be undone.

Contact

Email support@reaset.co. We aim to reply within two business days. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, or other secrets.
