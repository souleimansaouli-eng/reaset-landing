Reaset Support

Email support@reaset.co for help with Reaset. We aim to reply within two business days. Beta testers may also send feedback through TestFlight.

Common Requests

  • Account deletion: permanently delete your account from Settings in the app.
  • Data export: export reading data from Settings in the app.
  • Reading reminders: manage local reminder settings from the reminder settings screen.
  • Content reports: use Report beneath a circle message, or email support@reaset.co.
  • Privacy questions: review the Privacy Policy linked on this page.

Support Notes

Never include passwords, authentication codes, or other secrets. Include the app version and build number, device model, iOS version, what you were trying to do, what happened, and steps that reproduce the problem. For account help, include only the email address used for Reaset.
