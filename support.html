Common Requests

  • Account deletion
    Permanently delete your account from Settings in the app.
  • Data export
    Export your reading data from Settings in the app.
  • Reading reminders
    Manage local reminder settings from the reminder settings screen.
  • Content reports
    Use Report beneath a Circle message, or email us.
  • Privacy questions
    Review the Privacy Policy or contact support.

Support Notes

Never include passwords, authentication codes, or other secrets. Include the app version and build number, device model, iOS version, what you were trying to do, what happened, and steps that reproduce the problem. For account help, include only the email address used for Reaset.
