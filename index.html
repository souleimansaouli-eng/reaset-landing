A few things readers ask first.

What makes Reaset different from a book tracker?

Reaset combines book organization with small reading sessions, thoughtful progress, and recommendations shaped around your real-life reading moment.

Can I use Reaset for free?

Yes. Reaset has a free path, with optional Premium features for readers who want unlimited sessions, recommendation decks, and deeper insights.

Can I bring my existing reading list?

Yes. Reaset supports adding books and importing an existing list, so you do not need to rebuild your library one title at a time.
